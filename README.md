# Go Bear Career Task Teams

## Overview
**Go Bear Career Task Teams** is an interactive, web-based feature for the **Go Bear AI platform**, designed to help students and young professionals collaborate on **AI-powered career challenges**.  
It encourages engagement, teamwork, and skill development through structured tasks, progress tracking, and gamified rewards.

---

## Project Goals

### 1. Collaboration & Engagement
Allow users to create or join teams and work together on real-world, career-related challenges.

### 2. Task Management
Enable users to create, assign, and track tasks individually or within their teams.

### 3. Gamification
Integrate points, badges, and leaderboards to promote consistent participation and friendly competition.

### 4. User Experience
Follow **Go Bear AI’s design principles** for a clean, responsive, and intuitive interface.

### 5. Scalability
Design with growth in mind, supporting future features such as:
- Cross-team competitions  
- AI-driven career coaching  
- Analytics dashboards

---

## Key Features
-  **Team Creation & Management** — Users can form or join teams easily.  
-  **Task Boards** — Shared spaces for managing and assigning team challenges.  
-  **Progress Tracking** — Visual dashboards to monitor task completion and performance.  
-  **Gamified Rewards** — Points, badges, and leaderboards to recognize achievements.  
-  **Responsive Design** — Optimized for desktop and mobile experiences.

## System Architecture
1. **Frontend (Client)** — Handles UI, user interaction, and state management.  
2. **Backend (Server)** — Manages authentication, tasks, and team data.  
3. **Database** — Stores users, teams, tasks, and gamification metrics.
